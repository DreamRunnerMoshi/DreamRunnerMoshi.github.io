Research

I study how LLM systems behave when they do real work, and I test them against independent evaluation harnesses instead of their own reports. That work draws on more than ten years of building and operating large-scale distributed systems.

Current member of the Distributed Systems and Software Engineering (DSSE) Research Group, Institute of Information Technology, University of Dhaka, led by Dr. Kazi Muheymin-Us-Sakib.

LLM agents for software engineering

Emergent delegation between frontier and low-cost models, cost and accuracy trade-offs, and verification that does not trust self-reported success.

Bangla language AI

Benchmark datasets for evaluating LLMs in Bangla, and tokenizers designed to handle colloquial accent.

Distributed and geographic systems

Architectures for publishing and manipulating vector data on the web, and scalable backend systems in production.

Projects

2026 · Two-tier LLM agent architecture

Gru & Minion

A frontier model, Gru, owns the task end to end and chooses when to delegate bounded work to a cheap model, Minion. Gru re-runs the checks itself instead of trusting the agent’s report. I benchmarked it on the real SWE-bench and GAIA harnesses with model pairs from three vendors, and all three matched or beat their solo baseline on SWE-bench.
